Suggested way to study

  1. Days 1–90: work on Linux and networking fundamentals together; add the cybersecurity foundation lessons alongside them.
  2. Days 91–180: build stronger administration and troubleshooting skills, then practise defensive monitoring and web-security labs.
  3. Days 181–270: integrate the tracks through isolated VM labs, packet analysis, scripting, incident investigations, and secure configuration.
  4. Days 271–365: complete capstones and publish sanitized notes, diagrams, scripts, and reports as a portfolio.

The roadmap is a structured path, not a promise of expertise after one year. Repeating labs, reading documentation, debugging mistakes, and building original projects are what deepen skill.

Important lab rules

  • Take VirtualBox snapshots before major changes.
  • Use host-only or internal networking for isolated practice when appropriate.
  • Only test systems you own or have explicit permission to assess.
  • Keep notes and backups outside the VM; browser progress checkboxes are stored locally on that browser/device.
